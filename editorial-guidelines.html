Editorial Guidelines

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Pipariya Peoples is a digital news platform committed to delivering accurate, fair, and responsible journalism. These editorial guidelines outline the principles and standards that govern our news reporting, content creation, and editorial decision-making.

Our Mission: To serve the people of Pipariya, Narmadapuram, and Madhya Pradesh with trustworthy, timely, and impactful news — "Khabar Jo Maayne Rakhe" (News That Matters).

1. Accuracy and Fact-Checking

We are committed to publishing accurate and verified information:

  • All news stories are fact-checked before publication.
  • We verify information from multiple sources wherever possible.
  • Statistics, data, and claims are cross-referenced with official or credible sources.
  • If information cannot be independently verified, it is clearly attributed to its source or labeled as unconfirmed.

2. Source Verification and Attribution

We maintain strict standards for sourcing:

  • All reporting is based on first-hand accounts, official statements, or credible and verified sources.
  • We properly attribute quotes and statements to their original sources.
  • Anonymous sources are used only when necessary to protect the source's safety, and their credibility is verified internally.
  • We distinguish between news reporting, opinion pieces, and user-submitted content.

3. Fairness and Impartiality

Our editorial team strives for balanced and unbiased reporting:

  • We present multiple perspectives on issues of public interest.
  • We do not favor any political party, organization, or individual in our news reporting.
  • Opinion and analysis pieces are clearly labeled and separated from factual news reporting.
  • We give subjects of critical or investigative reporting an opportunity to respond before publication.

4. Corrections and Retractions

We take responsibility for errors in our reporting:

  • Factual errors are corrected promptly and transparently once identified.
  • Corrections are clearly noted within the article with the date of correction.
  • If a story is found to be substantially inaccurate, we issue a retraction with an explanation.
  • Readers can report errors or inaccuracies by contacting us at rajvardhanbaldua@gmail.com.

5. Independence and Conflicts of Interest

We maintain editorial independence:

  • Our editorial decisions are not influenced by advertisers, sponsors, or external commercial interests.
  • Journalists and editors must disclose any potential conflicts of interest.
  • Sponsored content and advertisements are clearly labeled and distinguished from editorial content.

6. Sensitivity and Ethical Standards

We report responsibly on sensitive topics:

  • We exercise caution when reporting on violence, crime, accidents, and tragedies, avoiding graphic or sensationalized content.
  • We respect the privacy and dignity of individuals, especially victims and minors.
  • We do not publish content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or communal disharmony.
  • We follow all applicable Indian laws and Press Council of India guidelines regarding media ethics.

7. Headlines and Presentation

We ensure our presentation of news is honest and not misleading:

  • Headlines accurately reflect the content of the article.
  • We do not use clickbait or misleading headlines to attract readers.
  • Thumbnails and preview images are representative of the actual story.

8. Multimedia Content

Standards for photos, videos, and audio:

  • All photos and videos used in reporting are authentic and unaltered (except for standard editing like cropping or brightness adjustment).
  • Stock images and file photos are clearly labeled as such.
  • We obtain proper permissions for copyrighted material.
  • User-submitted content is verified for authenticity before publication.

9. Community Content and User Contributions

We welcome community participation while maintaining quality:

  • User-submitted news tips and stories are reviewed and verified by our editorial team before publication.
  • We reserve the right to reject content that does not meet our editorial standards.
  • Community contributors are credited appropriately.

10. Editorial Team and Accountability

Our editorial operations are overseen by:

  • Editor-in-Chief: Rajvardhan Baldua
  • The Editor-in-Chief is responsible for all editorial decisions and ensuring adherence to these guidelines.
  • Our team is committed to ongoing training and improvement in journalistic standards.

11. Complaints and Feedback

We welcome feedback from our readers and are committed to addressing complaints:

  • Complaints regarding editorial content can be submitted to rajvardhanbaldua@gmail.com.
  • We aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 7 working days.
  • Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the Editor-in-Chief.

12. Updates to These Guidelines

These editorial guidelines may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or relevant regulations. The latest version will always be available on this page.
